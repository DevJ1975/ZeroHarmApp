The app is free

Zero Harm is free to download and use. Signing in is optional. We may add, change or remove features, or stop offering the app, at any time.

You must be at least 16 years old to use the app.

Safety information, not advice

The talks, sign-in sheets, alerts, weather and OTIS answers in Zero Harm are general safety information. They support your employer's safety training, written procedures and professional advice. They do not replace them. Always follow your employer's rules, your site's procedures, the equipment maker's instructions and the law.

Nothing in the app is legal, medical or compliance advice. Using the app does not make you or your company compliant with any law or standard. If you need advice, talk to a qualified professional.

Zero Harm is not affiliated with, endorsed by or part of OSHA or any government agency. When we quote or link to regulations, check the official source for the current text.

AI content can be wrong

The Monday Minute talk is written by AI and checked by an automated AI review before it is published. A person reviews it only in some weeks. OTIS is an AI assistant that runs on your phone. Both can be wrong, out of date or incomplete. Check anything important with your supervisor, your safety team or the official rules before you act on it. Never skip a safety step because the app said so.

Emergencies

In an emergency, call 911 (or your local emergency number) right away. Do not use the app to get emergency help. Weather alerts in the app may be late or missing, so watch official alerts too.

Community rules

  • Talk about the topic and your own site. Share what works and what you've seen.
  • No names or details that could identify an injured person, a coworker or a company.
  • No insults, harassment, threats, hate, sexual content or profanity aimed at anyone.
  • No ads, links, emails or phone numbers.
  • Don't tell others to skip a safety step. If you see something dangerous at work, stop and tell your supervisor.

You can report any comment or block any person. We act on every report within 24 hours. If a comment breaks these rules, we remove it and stop the person who posted it from commenting, without warning.
